CRIME
the homeowner in involved in illegal human traffixking/smuggling. he's beeing preparing to leave town because the police are 
tightening surveilance. his house contains traces of his involvement, but he's careful, so most of what you find is 
circumstantial unless you dig deeper.

EVIDENCE
1. Strong Evidence (must steal): physical objects that directly implicate him. riskier to obatain cuz he'll notice 
2. Circumstantial Evidence (can photograph): suspicious but not damning alone. builds a case when with stronger items
3. Red Herrings: looks suspicious at first but either have normal explanations or are explained by another item in the house

MUST STEAL EVIDENCE
1. Ledger hidden in drawer
    - handwritten notes with initials, dates, and amounts, looks like payments. some entries reference coded temrs like "cargo"
      and "transit" (photo isn't enough because it could get destroyed by homeowner later)
2. USB taped under table
    - contains encrypted files. if player finds computer later, can use it to see it contains a doc called "transport schedules"
      (must steal since photographing wont capture data)
3. Key with strange tag
    - A storage unit number written on it (Final "aha" clue. ties homeowner to offsite location where ppl/evidence is held)
      (must steal, photo of keys proves nothing)

PHOTO EVIDENCE
1. Large sum of cash in diffenrent curriencies
    - suspicious but could be explained as business dealings (photo cause will be noticed if stolen)
2. One way ticket to another country
    - country flagged for trafficking routes. shows intent to flee (photo because he'll notice if missing)
3. Handwritten note in foreign language   !!!!!!!!!!!!!!
    - when translated, says "shipment read by friday" reminder from an accomplice
4. Multiple burner phones 

RED HERRINGS
1. bloodstained shirt
    - suggests violence
    - actually from hunting trip. player can find hunting license and photos.
2. Prescription bottles with other peaoples names
    - suggest stolen meds or drug dealings
    - later you find a get well card from cousin thanking him for picking up prescriptions !!!!!!!!!!!!
3. rope and duct tape 
    - looks suspicious
    - later you find shipping boxes and furniture wrappings, showing its just for moving
4. Kitchen knife set with one missing
    - suggests violence
    - later you find the missing knife somewhere else in the house.

QUESTIONS TO ask
1. ask about bloodstained shirt - "that just from a hunting trip. I think i have some photos around here somewhere."
2. ask about burner phones - "i repair and resell them sometimes"
3. ask about foreign note - "oh just a recipe from a friend"
4. ask about rope and tape - "for packing up the furniture, obviously"

AT HOME
- photos pinned to corkboard
- stolen objects laid on the desk
- each pieve has a short description and maybe the homeowners explanation attatch if the player asked for it

EVIDENCE SORTING
- three folders: Hard evidence, Circumstantial, Not relevent
- drag each piece into the folder they think it belongs in
- Accuracy = how well they categorize items.

ANSWER QUESTIONS
- after reviewing evidence, game asks:
    - "What was the homeowners crime?" 
    - "Which 3 pieces prove it beyond doubt?" (ledger, USB, key)
    - "Which items were distractions?"
- answers determine the ending.

GOOD ENDING (90-100%)
- player chooses ledger, USB, and ley as primary proof AND correctly discarded most red Herrings
NEUTRAL ENDING (50-89%)
- player only identified some real evidence but got distracted by red herrings / missed key pieces
BAD ENDING (0-49%)
- player failed to highlight true core evidence or leaned too heavily on red herrings
